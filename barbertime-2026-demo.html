Yes. Walk-ins are welcome at both shops. Booking a time just skips the wait.

Yes. Our barbers are patient with little ones, including kids who find the chair hard.
Can I book from an Android phone?
Yes, right on this page. There are also iPhone and Android app links in the booking section.

Prices are added once the owner confirms them. Call your shop and they will tell you.
