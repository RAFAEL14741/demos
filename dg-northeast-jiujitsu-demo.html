The belt
journey.

Jiu-jitsu is a long game. This is the path every student walks, and the way this page moves as you scroll.

Rank 01White

Start here. Walk in, meet the room, learn the first moves. Reviewers say newcomers feel welcome.

Rank 02Blue

Learn the fundamentals until they stop feeling new: posture, escapes, control.

Rank 03Purple

Grow your own game. Connect techniques, find what fits your body.

Rank 04Brown

Sharpen under pressure. For those who want to compete, and those who simply love hard rounds.

Rank 05Black

Teach it forward. Mastery is passing on what the mat gave you.

The belt path is a storytelling frame for this page and the general BJJ tradition. It is not a statement about how this academy ranks or tests students.
