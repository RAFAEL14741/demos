01 — What we do

Kitchens & baths, Mastered.

Two rooms carry most of a home's daily life and most of its resale value. We only build those — and the spaces that connect to them.

Kitchen remodeling

The room you actually live in.

Rowhome galley or open-plan addition, we design kitchens around how you cook, host, and pass through — then build them with our own crew. Cabinetry, counters, tile, lighting, plumbing and electrical, all under one contract.

  • Custom & semi-custom cabinetry
  • Quartz, granite & butcher block
  • Backsplash & floor tile
  • Islands & peninsulas
  • Lighting & electrical
  • Plumbing & appliance hookup

02 — How it works

No guesswork. Just a plan.

One contract, one crew, one number. Here's what the six weeks between "we should redo this" and "come see it" actually look like.

  1. Day 101

    Walkthrough & listen

    We come to you, measure everything, and hear how you actually use the space before we suggest a thing.

  2. Week 102

    Design & fixed quote

    A layout you can see, materials you can touch, and one written number — no allowances that balloon later.

  3. Weeks 2–603

    Build, clean, update

    Our own crew, dust walls up, floors protected, and a text every evening with what got done and what's next.

  4. Done04

    Final walk & warranty

    Punch list cleared before we call it finished, and a written workmanship warranty you'll never need to find.

03 — Our story

Family-run. Philly proud.

FMA Constructions started in 2015 with one truck, a tile saw, and a lot of Rhawnhurst kitchens that needed more than a coat of paint. Ten years on, we're still a small, family-run crew — the same people who quote your job are the ones who show up to build it.

We don't sub out the parts that matter. Tile, plumbing, cabinetry and finish carpentry are done by our own team, on a schedule we put in writing before the first day of demo.

  • Licensed & insured in Pennsylvania
  • Fixed-price quotes — no allowance surprises
  • In-house crew for tile, plumbing & carpentry
  • Daily clean-up and dust control
  • Written workmanship warranty
