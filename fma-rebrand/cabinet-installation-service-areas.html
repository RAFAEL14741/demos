Cabinet installation near you

FMA Constructions installs and refaces kitchen cabinets for homeowners across Northeast Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs. Same crew, same standard on every job: a clean jobsite, one point of contact, and a free on-site estimate before you commit to anything.

Whether it is new cabinets for a remodel, a custom island, or existing cabinets that just need a new look, we come out, take real measurements, and give you a straight answer on cost and timeline.

Where we work

  • Northeast Philadelphia: Fox Chase, Rhawnhurst, Bustleton, Somerton, Holmesburg, Tacony, Mayfair, Oxford Circle
  • Bucks County: Newtown, Feasterville, Langhorne, Warminster and surrounding townships
  • Montgomery County: Abington, Willow Grove, Huntingdon Valley, Jenkintown, Horsham
  • Main Line, PA
  • New Jersey: call or text to confirm your town

Do not see your town listed? Call or text us. We cover most of the Philadelphia suburbs and can confirm quickly.

How the process works

  1. Call, text or fill out the form. We respond within one business day.
  2. Free on-site walkthrough: we measure, look at your cabinets, and talk through what you want.
  3. A clear, itemized estimate before any work starts.
  4. Installation with a clean jobsite every day, start to finish.
  5. A final walkthrough to make sure everything's right before we call it done.

Questions

What areas does FMA Constructions serve?

We install and reface kitchen cabinets throughout Northeast Philadelphia, Bucks County, Montgomery County and the Main Line, PA, and we take New Jersey jobs too. If you're not sure whether we cover your town, call or text us.

Is the estimate free everywhere in your service area?

Yes, the on-site walkthrough and estimate are free anywhere in our service area.

How far in advance should I book a project?

It depends on the season and scope, but reach out as soon as you are planning a kitchen project so we can get your free walkthrough on the calendar and give you a realistic start date.

Are you licensed and insured everywhere you work?

Yes, FMA Constructions is licensed and insured to work throughout Pennsylvania.
