The shop

The neighborhood’s place for a cone.

Frozen Wonders is an independent ice cream shop in the Mayfair section, a short walk from Chickie & Pete’s. The owners describe it as the spot where locals meet friends for a cone, a milkshake or one of their unique cold treats.
