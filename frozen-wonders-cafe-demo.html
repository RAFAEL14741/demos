The shop

The neighborhood’s place for a cone.

Frozen Wonders is an independent ice cream shop in the Mayfair section, a short walk from Chickie & Pete’s. The owners describe it as the spot where locals meet friends for a cone, a milkshake or one of their unique cold treats.

Quick answers

Before you visit.

Where is it?

4100 Robbins St, Philadelphia, PA 19135, in Mayfair.

When is it open?

Per Yelp: 3 PM to 9 PM Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday, 3 PM to 10 PM Friday and Saturday. Closed Wednesday. The owner confirms before we publish.

What do they serve?

Ice cream, milkshakes, bubble waffles, water ice and pretzels.

Can I ask about a group or party?

Yes. Use the Plan a visit form on this page and pick Group or party treats. Party offers are added once the owner confirms.
