Our story

Your corner
bakery.

Holmesburg Bakery is a longtime family-owned neighborhood bakery on Frankford Avenue. It’s the place people go for the everyday things: the donuts for the office, the danish for Sunday, the pie for the table.

And when the day is bigger, there are custom cakes for birthdays and weddings.
