The promise

If it’s not right, it doesn’t go out.

Joanie’s says it takes pride in the quality of the meat on every hoagie, and that an order that isn’t just the way you asked for it won’t be sent out.

The menu runs from old-school hoagies to custom builds, with sides on the side.
