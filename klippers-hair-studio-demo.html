More than a haircut.
A Fox Chase staple.

Klipper's Hair Studio has been a fixture on Rhawn Street for years, run by a genuinely family team — clients mention Robert, Doris and Gina by name because they've been cutting the same neighborhood's hair for that long. It's bright, it's clean, and it's the kind of place where you're greeted like you've been coming in for years, even on your first visit.

Men's cuts, women's color and balayage, keratin smoothing treatments, blowouts, and kids' cuts — all in the same easygoing, no-rush chair Fox Chase has trusted for decades. 130 Google reviews and a 4.7-star average say the rest.
