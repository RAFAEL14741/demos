Our Story

A Fox Chase Staple, Chair by Chair

Klipper's Hair Studio has been a fixture on Rhawn Street for years, run by a genuinely family team — clients mention Robert, Doris and Gina by name because they've been cutting the same neighborhood's hair for that long.

It's bright, it's clean, and it's the kind of place where you're greeted like you've been coming in for years — even on your first visit. Men's cuts, women's color and styling, keratin treatments, and everything in between.
