"Here, no appointment is ever rushed — every nail gets exactly the time it deserves."

L A Nails Salon sits on Bustleton Ave in Northeast Philadelphia, where regulars keep coming back for one simple reason: nobody feels rushed out the door. Our techs take their time on every hand and every foot, whether you're in for a quick gel fill-in or a full liquid gel set before a big event.

We also keep one of the largest physical design books around — gel and dip styles well beyond what fits on a phone screen. Come with a picture in mind, or page through the book with your tech and leave with something you didn't expect.
