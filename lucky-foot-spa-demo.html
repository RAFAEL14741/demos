The reflexology map

Where does your day live in your feet?

Reflexology traditions map areas of the foot to parts of the body. Tap a zone to see what it is traditionally associated with, then choose a focus for your visit.

Start here

Tap a numbered zone.

Each area of the foot is traditionally linked to a part of the body. Pick one that matches how you feel today and we will pass it to your booking.

Reflexology is a relaxation practice based on tradition. It is not a medical treatment and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

The ritual

Four quiet movements.

A visit is meant to feel like a slow exhale, from the door to the last few minutes of rest.

i.

Arrive

Step in from Rhawn Street to warm lighting, soft music and a friendly hello.

ii.

Soak

Settle in and let the feet warm and soften before the work begins.

iii.

Reflexology

Steady, thoughtful pressure across the zones of the foot, at a level you choose.

iv.

Rest

A few still minutes before you go. Sip water, breathe, step back out lighter.

Illustrative flow. The exact steps for each service are confirmed by the spa at booking.
