A hidden gem worth finding
on Grant Ave.

Marakanda Restaurant brings authentic Uzbekistan cuisine to Bustleton — the kind of food that tastes like it came straight from a home kitchen in Tashkent. Hand-folded dumplings, kebabs grilled the traditional way, and a menu built around recipes passed down, not trends. First-time guests keep saying the same thing: they had no idea Uzbek food was this good, and they didn't know it existed just off Grant Ave.

The hospitality is what people remember most — walk in once and the staff treats you like family from then on.
