A Bustleton Ave staple.

New England Pizza has become one of the most-reviewed pizza spots on Bustleton Ave — 300+ Google reviews and counting. Neighbors call in for a Friday-night pie, a stack of buffalo wings, or a hoagie that actually holds up on the ride home.

Cheese pizza, pepperoni pizza, wings by the ten-piece, baked ziti, chicken parm, cheesesteaks and hoagies — the whole rotation of Philly neighborhood-pizzeria staples, made to order and ready for pickup.
