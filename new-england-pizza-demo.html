Our Story

A Neighborhood Kitchen
On Bustleton Ave

New England Pizza is a family-owned pizzeria serving Bustleton Ave with dine-in, takeout and delivery. The menu keeps it straightforward: thick-crust pizza, saucy wings, Sunday-style pasta and hoagies stacked the way they should be.

No frills, no shortcuts — just a neighborhood spot that answers the phone, gets the order right, and has it ready when you say it will be.
