About

A family bakeryon Frankford Avenue.

Nonno Ugo's is family-owned and a neighborhood staple for Tacony and Mayfair. Some regulars say it has been around since they were kids. It sits in a small strip next to a dry cleaner and a Dunkin. The sign is small, so look for the lot out front.

Bread and rolls are the most-praised thing in reviews, followed by tomato pie and stromboli. The tomato pie has also been named in a guide to Philadelphia's best.
