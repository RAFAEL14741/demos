Our Story

A Northeast Philly Landmark for Over 37 Years

What started as a small breakfast counter in 1987 grew into the most beloved diner on Roosevelt Blvd. Three generations of loyal regulars. The same recipes. The same warmth. Never compromised.

We source fresh seafood weekly, bake our own bread, and make every soup from scratch daily. When you sit down at the Ritz, you're not a customer — you're family.
