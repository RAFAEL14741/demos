Tiny dancers welcome

A first stage for little dancers.

Every dancer begins somewhere. At Tina’s Center Stage in Mayfair, that first stage is a friendly neighborhood studio, run by Miss Tina, with weekly combination classes for dancers ages 3 and up.

Whether your child is stepping into a first class, celebrating with a themed birthday party, or spending part of the summer at camp, this is a place to move, play and shine.
