More than a haircut.
A whole family's spot.

Tucked inside The Shops At Red Lion on Bustleton Ave, Top Cut Hair Salon has built a loyal following one appointment at a time. Every single wash here comes with a relaxing head-and-back massage — it's not an upsell, it's just how we do things. Bring the kids, walk in without an appointment, and expect the same warm welcome every visit.

From a first haircut for a nervous ten-year-old to a quick trim on a busy Saturday, Top Cut keeps it simple: friendly service, real skill, and a chair that never feels rushed.
